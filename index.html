Quick answers

Common questions,
clearly answered.

Need something else? Our support team is one message away.

Do I need an account to register or book?+

No. Candidate registration, availability and interview booking work without a login. Email verification keeps the workflow secure.

Can I have two interviews on the same day?+

Yes. A candidate may book multiple interviews on the same day—even with matching details—when panel capacity is available.

What happens when an admin blocks a slot?+

The blocked panel or time disappears from live availability. The server checks again before saving so stale pages cannot take a blocked slot.

Are weekends available?+

Saturday and Sunday are closed by default. A special-open exception may be published by the admin for a specific weekend date.

Clear by design

Privacy and acceptable use

We collect only the details needed to register candidates, coordinate interviews and provide career support.

How records are used

Candidate and booking details are stored in the organization’s private Google Workspace and used by authorized SK Tech staff for service delivery.

Analytics boundaries

Google Analytics loads only after consent. Personal details, SK IDs, booking references and form answers are never included in analytics events.

Your responsibility

Submit genuine, accurate information and keep email verification codes and booking manage tokens private. Contact support to request a correction.
